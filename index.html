Capabilities

Everything you need to secure your supply chain.

From scanning a single project on your laptop to continuous monitoring across your entire organization.

🔍

Multi-Engine Scanning

8 detection engines run in parallel: OSV, Trivy, Grype, Semgrep, behavioral analysis, malware signatures, typosquatting, and AI model safety checks.

🧠

AI-Native Triage

Claude-powered analysis explains findings in plain language, assesses real-world exploitability, and generates patch suggestions automatically.

📝

Community Signatures

Open YAML-based detection rules. Contribute signatures for new threats — blocklists, behavioral patterns, typosquatting, and MCP tool risks.

📦

SBOM + Provenance

Generate SPDX and CycloneDX SBOMs. Cryptographically sign artifacts with SLSA Level 3 provenance. Know exactly what shipped.

📊

Web Dashboard

Full-featured React UI with real-time scan progress, findings table, dependency graphs, risk heatmaps, and policy management.

🔒

Local-First Privacy

Everything runs on your infrastructure. No dependency data leaves your network unless you explicitly enable AI features. Zero vendor lock-in.
